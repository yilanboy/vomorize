# Surface Brief: Curriculum Levels Directory (resources/js/pages/Levels.svelte)

## Scope and Mode

- Surface: `resources/js/pages/Levels.svelte`
- Mode: Operate

## Visitor Context

- Audience: English learners navigating the 7-level vocabulary curriculum (7,000 words total), reviewing their progress per level, and entering target levels.
- Action/Goal: Scan difficulty levels, inspect mastery progress, and enter a specific level group matrix.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A clean, structured 7-level curriculum overview directory that displays real SRS mastery percentages, words count, and learning paths with high scanability and tactile card feedback, refusing cluttered educational tropes.
OWN-WORLD: Inter typography with tabular figures, Slate/Zinc neutral surfaces, Primary Blue (#2563eb) accents, Mastery Emerald (#059669) progress indicators, min 14px text-sm, zero purple/violet.
STORY: The learner arrives at the curriculum index, immediately sees the 7,000-word difficulty progression, checks their individual level mastery rate and progress bar, and selects a level to practice.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Clear page title and subtitle, global curriculum overview stats, and a responsive grid of curriculum cards with level badge, mastery percentage, words count, and emerald progress bar.
FORM: Modern Curriculum Level Directory (Operate mode)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
