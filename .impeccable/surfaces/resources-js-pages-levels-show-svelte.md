# Surface Brief: Level Detail Group Matrix (resources/js/pages/levels/Show.svelte)

## Scope and Mode

- Surface: `resources/js/pages/levels/Show.svelte`
- Mode: Operate

## Visitor Context

- Audience: Language learners managing their daily vocabulary curriculum, tracking unit review states, and launching learning sessions.
- Action/Goal: Scan 100 groups across the current level, filter by review urgency (Ready, Cooldown, Not Started, Completed), and launch unit study or review.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A high-density, tactile 100-group vocabulary mastery matrix for Level Detail, turning SRS progress and review availability into an instantly scanable operational dashboard without decorative clutter.
OWN-WORLD: Inter typography with tabular figures, Slate/Zinc neutral surfaces, Primary Blue (#2563eb) for ready units, Mastery Emerald (#059669) for completed mastery, warm Amber for cooldown, subtle borders, min 14px text-sm, zero purple/violet.
STORY: The learner inspects their current level's 100 units, filters by urgency (ready to review, in cooldown, unstarted, completed), visually reads unit numbers and status icons at a glance, and clicks to immediately launch new learning or SRS recall review.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Clear level title and description with stats counter, responsive filter pill bar with live count badges, and the opening tiers of the 10x10 matrix grid with distinct, high-contrast status states.
FORM: Cognitive Mastery Instrument Matrix (Operate mode)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
